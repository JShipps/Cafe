Our story

Slow bread for a fast world

We started with one wood-fired oven and a sourdough starter named Gus. Ten years on, Gus is still going — and so is our promise: everything is made by hand, from scratch, in the room behind the counter.

We buy flour from a family mill two hours north, eggs from Willow Creek Farm and dairy from Riverton Creamery. What we don't sell by close goes to the Riverton Community Pantry.
